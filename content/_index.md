I write about education data, program evaluation, and the policy decisions they shape, drawing on a doctorate in causal inference and education policy, years as a classroom teacher and instructional coach, and my current work leading data, assessment, and reporting systems for a California school district. I pair research rigor with the practical realities of running data at scale, in service of better decisions that affect families, students, and schools.
