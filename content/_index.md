This site collects my writing on education data, program evaluation, and the policy decisions they shape. I write from several vantage points: a doctorate in causal inference and education policy, years as a classroom teacher and instructional coach, and now leading data, assessment, and reporting systems for a California school district. The posts here mix research rigor with the practical realities of running data at scale, always in service of better decisions that affect families, students, and schools.
